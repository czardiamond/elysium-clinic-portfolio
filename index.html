Honest scope

What we’re exploring — and what we won’t pretend to solve.

The best starting point is a shared understanding of the edges.

Within the proposal

  • Clinic-approved FAQs and response drafts
  • Human-reviewed appointment requests
  • Follow-up on missed enquiries
  • A simple enquiry log and weekly summary

Outside the scope

  • Diagnosis, treatment advice, or deciding urgency
  • Clinical triage or autonomous bookings
  • Requesting patient information
  • Promises about revenue, speed, savings, demand, or results

A few useful answers

Before we make anything real.

Is the clinic workflow live?

No. This page presents Elysium’s first proposed solution, an early-stage exploration by Diamond Anyanwu. There are no active clinic integrations, real clinic accounts, or proven outcomes.

Can someone book an appointment here?

No. The interaction above is a local-only illustration. Nothing is saved or sent, and an appointment request would never equal a confirmed appointment.

What is Elysium?

Elysium is an independent AI consultancy. The clinic enquiry workflow shown here is its first proposed solution; it remains exploratory and has not been deployed.

Who is the first proposal for?

Independent dental clinics in Lagos who want to think carefully about patient-access administration, with their own team retaining responsibility.

What happens if we talk?

We can discuss your current context, approved information, and what should remain firmly human. It is a discovery conversation, not a sales or patient intake form.
