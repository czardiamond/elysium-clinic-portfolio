Honest scope

Clear scope. People in charge.

Start with the administrative work worth improving; leave care decisions with the clinical team.

Designed for administrative support

  • Clinic-approved answers and response drafts
  • Appointment requests prepared for staff review
  • Clear follow-up flags for missed enquiries
  • A simple log and weekly view of open work

Care decisions stay with the team

  • No diagnosis, treatment advice, or clinical urgency decisions
  • No clinical triage or autonomous bookings
  • No patient information requested in this concept
  • Staff approve every reply and confirm every appointment

Clear answers. Confident next steps.

Is the clinic workflow live?

Not yet. Elysium’s first concept is exploratory and has not been deployed. This page shows a synthetic interface, not client work; any next step would begin with a discovery conversation and a clearly scoped design.

Can someone book an appointment here?

No. This illustrative demo has no clinic connection and cannot check availability or confirm appointments. The concept shows how requests could be prepared for staff review.

What is Elysium?

Elysium is an independent AI consultancy by Diamond Anyanwu, focused on practical, human-centered applications of AI. The clinic enquiry workflow is its first proposed concept.

Who is the first proposal for?

Independent dental clinics in Lagos exploring a clearer administrative enquiry process while keeping their own team in control.

What happens if we talk?

We can understand your goals and current workflow, identify where AI could help, and decide whether a focused exploration makes sense. It is a clinic-business discovery conversation, not patient intake.
